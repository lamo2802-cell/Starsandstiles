Built by owners

We’re holiday let owners too.

Stars & Stiles started with our own holiday cottage, Cringley Cottage, in the Yorkshire Dales.

Like most owners, we wanted more direct bookings and less reliance on third-party platforms and their fees. However, we also knew that taking bookings ourselves meant more admin, more things to keep on top of and more time spent managing the business alongside our full-time jobs and young family.

So, we built the solution we wanted for ourselves.

A website that takes bookings and payments, keeps calendars in sync and makes running our holiday let simpler in so many other ways. It works for us every day, and now we build the same solution for other owners.

No complicated tech, no starting from scratch, just a straightforward way to take more control of your bookings, leaving you more time for the things that matter most.

Compliance Watch

There’s enough to keep on top of when you run a holiday let without having to keep checking what’s changed.

With Compliance Watch, we keep an eye on the rules and requirements that affect holiday let owners and explain everything you need to know in plain English. Where it affects your website, we can even make the changes for you.

One less thing on your list, from £9.99 a month.

Updates in plain English

What changed, who it affects and what owners commonly need to think about, with links to the official source.
