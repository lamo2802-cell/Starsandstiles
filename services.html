What we do

Two services, one goal

Helping holiday let owners take more control, keep more of their booking income and spend less time on the day-to-day.

Core service

Your very own booking website

A beautiful, fully functioning website built around your holiday let. Take bookings directly, keep more of what your guests pay and let the clever bits take care of the admin.

What’s included

  • A beautiful, bespoke website that reflects your property and its personality
  • Availability calendar that syncs with Airbnb, Booking.com and other compatible platforms
  • Instant quotes with flexible seasonal pricing and minimum stays
  • Secure online payments, including deposits and balance collection
  • Automated guest emails, from booking confirmations and payment reminders to arrival information and check-out
  • Your own space to manage bookings, prices and availability
  • Guest information, house guides and all those useful little details in one place
  • Your own domain name, registered in your name
  • The choice to manage your website yourself or leave the hosting, updates and support to us
Optional extra

Compliance Watch

Keeping up with changing rules and regulations probably isn’t your favourite part of running a holiday let. That’s where we come in.

From £9.99 a month, we’ll help you stay one step ahead.

  • Clear, plain-English updates when relevant rules and regulations change
  • Links to official guidance, so you know where the information comes from
  • Website updates taken care of when changes affect your site
  • Help keeping your reviews policy, pricing information and guest pages up to date
  • Optional safety and changeover checklists to help you stay organised
  • A straightforward owner checklist highlighting things worth reviewing

Less time researching the rules. More peace of mind.

Two ways to run your site

Every site is built the same way. What happens after launch is up to you, and you can switch later.

Option 1

We build it, you run it

We design and build the site, set everything up in your own accounts, show you how it all works, and hand it over. It is yours to manage.

  • Domain, hosting and accounts in your name
  • A walkthrough of how to run it day to day
  • Optional support or Compliance Watch, whenever you want it
Option 2

We manage it

We keep the site hosted, secure and up to date, update prices and content when you ask, and look after the technical side, so you can get on with welcoming guests.

  • Hosting, security and updates included
  • Changes made for you
  • Support when something needs fixing
